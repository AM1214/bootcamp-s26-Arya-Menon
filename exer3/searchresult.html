What is a Sloth?

Talents

Sloths are surprisingly excellent swimmers, using a doggy-paddle motion to cross rivers in the wild. Their powerful, hook-like grip also allows them to hang upside down for hours without wasting any energy.

Where they Live

Sloths spend nearly their entire lives living in the tropical rainforests of Central and South America, rarely leaving the safety of the trees.

What they Eat

They eat high up in the canopy of the rainforest, munching on leaves, twigs, and buds.
